Privacy Policy for RoadGrid Pro

Effective Date: October 2026

RoadGrid Pro ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles your data.

1. Zero Server Data Collection

RoadGrid Pro operates on a local-first architecture. All your vehicle logs, trip histories, fuel calculations, service records, and uploaded document images are stored locally on your device's SQLite/Room database. We do not operate remote servers to store, sell, or process your personal data.

2. Permissions Requested & How They Are Used

  • Location Access (GPS): Used solely to provide real-time speed display on the HUD Speedometer and calculate trip distances. Location data is processed on-device and is never transmitted to external servers.
  • Camera & Storage: Used strictly when you scan or capture images of vehicle documents (RC, Insurance, PUC) for your local Document Vault.
  • Notifications: Used to dispatch local system alerts for upcoming vehicle service due dates and document expiry reminders.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

RoadGrid Pro uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identification data (Advertising ID) and network diagnostics strictly for ad delivery and fraud prevention in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

4. User Data Control & Deletion (GDPR / CCPA)

You have full ownership of your data. You can erase all vehicle records, trip logs, and cached files at any time using the "Delete All Local Data" button inside the app's Dashboard Settings menu. Uninstalling the app permanently purges all local app data from your device.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our developer support channel or repository.
